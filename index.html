EraseX – Account Deletion

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

This page explains how users of EraseX can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Developer: Defanzo Developers

How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your EraseX account and the personal data associated with it, send a deletion request to the email address below.

Account Deletion Request
Request Account Deletion

orion.apps.team@gmail.com

To help us locate your account, please send the request from the same Google account/email address associated with your EraseX account whenever possible.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete applicable personal information associated with your EraseX account, which may include:

  • EraseX account information
  • Firebase user account information
  • Account identifiers associated with your EraseX account
  • Other personal data associated with your account that is under our control

Image Data

Images uploaded to EraseX for processing are used to provide the requested image-processing functionality.

Temporary image-processing files are intended to be deleted when they are no longer necessary for processing the requested operation, subject to the retention practices of the relevant third-party processing service.

We do not sell user-uploaded images.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained when required or permitted by applicable law, for example:

  • Information required for legal or regulatory obligations
  • Transaction or purchase records required for accounting or legal purposes
  • Information necessary to prevent fraud or abuse
  • Information required to resolve disputes or enforce legal agreements

Where data is retained for these purposes, it will be retained only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose.

Processing Time

We will review and process valid account deletion requests within a reasonable period after verifying the request and the identity of the account owner.

Important Information

Please note: Deleting your EraseX account may permanently remove your account information and associated data. This action may not be reversible.

Deleting your EraseX account does not necessarily delete transaction records or other information that must be retained by Google Play, payment providers, or other third-party services according to their own policies and legal obligations.
